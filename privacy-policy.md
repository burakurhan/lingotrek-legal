# LingoTrek Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction

LingoTrek ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application LingoTrek.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Learning preferences and onboarding data (language level, goals)
- In-app purchase information (processed by Apple App Store)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- App usage data (puzzles completed, levels played, streak data)
- Device information (iOS version, device model)
- Crash reports and performance data (via Firebase Crashlytics)
- Analytics data (via Firebase Analytics)

### 2.3 AI Features
- When using AI-powered features, your learning context (CEFR level, weak topics) is sent to our AI provider to generate personalized content
- Conversation data from AI Travel Companion is not stored on our servers

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve the LingoTrek experience
- To personalize your learning journey
- To track your progress and achievements
- To send push notifications (only with your permission)
- To process in-app purchases
- To analyze app performance and fix bugs

## 4. Data Storage

- Your learning progress is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage
- Analytics and crash data is stored on Firebase servers (Google Cloud)
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties

## 5. Third-Party Services

LingoTrek uses the following third-party services:
- **Firebase** (Google) — Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config
- **RevenueCat** — Subscription management
- **Google AdMob** — Advertising (free tier only)
- **Apple App Store** — In-app purchases

## 6. Children's Privacy

LingoTrek is suitable for users aged 4 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data stored in the app (via Profile screen)
- Delete your data (by deleting the app)
- Opt out of analytics (via device settings)
- Disable push notifications (via device settings)

## 8. Data Retention

Local app data is retained until you delete the app. Analytics data is retained according to Firebase's data retention policies (up to 14 months).

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
